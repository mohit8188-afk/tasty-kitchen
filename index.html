Good to know

Simple answers before you order.

Are the sweets made fresh?

Yes. Tasty Kitchen is using a confirmed-order model: payment is confirmed first, then the batch is prepared fresh for the scheduled delivery.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Initial launch is focused on selected Ahmedabad societies including Shree Balaji Wind Park, Gala Heaven and Adani Shantigram. Wider delivery can be added later.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes. Quantity-based festive gifting orders are planned, with a separate bulk discount ladder to be finalized.

What are the ingredients in Makhana Mewa Bites?

Makhana, dates, cashew, almond, raisins, desiccated coconut, ghee and a limited amount of added sugar.

How should I store the sweets?

Storage and best-use guidance will be printed on the final packaging once final product validation and compliance labeling are complete.

How do I pay?

All orders are prepaid. The live checkout will support UPI and cards through a secure payment gateway. You will not need to type a UPI reference number or send a payment screenshot.

Can I place an order without paying?

No. Tasty Kitchen does not hold production slots against unpaid orders. This keeps the small-batch production schedule accurate.

What if Tasty Kitchen cannot fulfil a paid order?

A clear cancellation and refund policy will be published before launch. At minimum, the live policy should explain what happens if Tasty Kitchen is unable to fulfil a prepaid order.

Does the product contain allergens?

Makhana Mewa Bites contain cashew and almond and use ghee. Coconut Mewa Ladoo preview content includes coconut, cashew, almond and ghee. Final packaging and the live website will carry the approved ingredient and allergen declaration for each product.
